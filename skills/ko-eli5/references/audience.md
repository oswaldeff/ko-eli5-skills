# Fixed audience

Edit this file to change who the report is written for. The skill reads it; it never detects the audience
from the prompt. (In upstream ELI5 the audience is detected per request and defaults to "Age 5". That
default is removed here.)

## Reader

The Korean-speaking owner of this project. Technical background (software developer), but reading this
report **as a decision-maker**, often on limited time.

## Frame

Borrowed from ELI5's "Manager" row: impact, timeline, risk, cost, and *what decision is needed now*.

## Register

Borrowed from ELI5's "Engineer" row: proper terminology is welcome; the reader is not patronized by precise
terms. Use the Korean term from `glossary.md`; put the English term in parentheses on first use only.
Sentence endings: -해요/-어요.

## What the reader wants first

1. Is it done or not.
2. What was confirmed vs. what is still a guess.
3. What they need to decide.
4. What got tried and dropped, so they don't suggest it again.

## What the reader does not want

Implementation narration, adjectives, reassurance, meta-narration ("이제 설명하자면").

## Non-negotiables regardless of audience

- The status word and the `[확인]/[추정]/[가정]` labels are reproduced at 100% fidelity. Only prose around them
  may be simplified.
- The English source is attached verbatim.
